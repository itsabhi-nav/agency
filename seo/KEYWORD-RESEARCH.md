# Keyword research

**Research date:** 2026-11-15 · **Market:** India · **Languages queried:** English (India) and Hindi (India)

## Important limitations

Google Autocomplete is a discovery source, not a keyword-volume tool. Search volume, difficulty, CPC, and conversion rates are **unavailable** here. Treat phrases below as observed suggestions or clearly labelled intent hypotheses—not verified demand forecasts. Validate with Google Ads Keyword Planner and later with Search Console.

## Observed autocomplete evidence

Google Autocomplete research in India surfaced broad work-from-home queries, including “work from home jobs for women India” and “best work from home jobs for women in India,” along with a broad Hindi query around “घर से काम महिलाओं के लिए”. These are discovery observations only; no suggestion list or numeric demand data is preserved.

The tested Stripchat-specific agency/model terms returned no suggestions in the research snapshot. This is not a volume estimate and may vary by time, location, or personalization.

## Keyword use guidance

- Keep the home page's visible, natural description of the Stripchat agency in India. It already aligns with the most relevant branded intent.
- Keep `/apply` focused on joining requirements and next steps, and `/help` focused on safety/support.
- Use terms like “adult creator” and “18+” transparently. Avoid describing this as a generic employment vacancy or adding `JobPosting` markup.
- Do not add a meta-keywords tag, hidden keyword text, city doorway pages, or repeated phrase lists.
- Confirm business claims and platform terminology before expanding keyword-targeted copy.

# Backlink and authority plan

**Status:** research framework only. No links have been acquired and no outreach has been sent.

## Safe, relevant opportunity types

| Opportunity type | Potential value | Approach | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official platform creator resources | High relevance and factual alignment | Ask the platform whether it maintains agency/creator resource listings and what its requirements are. | Do not imply endorsement or pay for a followed link. |
| Existing creator education and safety resources | Audience relevance | Offer a genuinely useful, fact-checked privacy/safety explainer to a legitimate publisher. | No copied, thin, or promotional-only guest post. |
| Relevant professional/community partnerships | Qualified awareness | Build real relationships and disclose any sponsorship/relationship. | Do not use fake profiles, hidden paid links, or mass messaging. |
| Local/industry business listings | Possible entity consistency | Submit only to legitimate directories that accept the actual business category and verified details. | No fake office address, category, or location. |
| Original useful resources | Long-term editorial citations | Publish sourced guides/checklists after confirming operational facts and legal review. | Do not invent statistics, surveys, or case studies. |

## Do not do

- Buy bulk links, use private blog networks, automated comment/profile links, or link exchanges.
- Pay for followed links without proper disclosure/qualification.
- Generate fake reviews, brand mentions, directory citations, or competitor comparisons.
- Create location doorway pages to attract unrelated local searches.

## Qualification checklist

Before any outreach, document: topical fit, editorial ownership, real audience, traffic relevance, link placement/context, sponsorship disclosure, and whether a link is editorially earned. All outreach requires human approval.

# SEO growth strategy

**Purpose:** build sustainable, relevant visibility for the agency's actual India-focused adult-creator onboarding service. Rankings are not guaranteed.

## Positioning and page roles

1. **Home (`/`)** — branded agency discovery and a factual overview of the offer.
2. **Apply (`/apply`)** — transactional next steps, clearly stating eligibility and what happens to submitted details.
3. **Help (`/help`)** — safety, privacy, payment and support questions, with limits explained.

Do not create overlapping landing pages for every phrase. Use real Search Console queries to decide whether a page has a distinct audience and unanswered need.

## Priorities

### Before launch

- Use an HTTPS domain controlled by the publishing organization in `SITE_URL`.
- Optionally set `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL` to a working mailbox controlled by the organization; leave blank to omit email contact from the site.
- Confirm domain DNS/SSL, redirects, canonical host, privacy policy, contact mailbox, and lead-form data handling.
- Review every strong public claim with the business owner and remove or qualify unsupported claims.
- Make the agency/platform relationship explicit; do not imply a platform-owned or official signup service without proof.

### First 30 days after launch

- Verify the final domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit sitemap; inspect home, apply, and help URLs.
- Confirm analytics only after the privacy/consent approach is approved.
- Record baseline indexed URLs, impressions, clicks, CTR, inquiries and traffic source.

### First 90 days

- Prioritize queries/pages with impressions and useful conversion intent.
- Improve snippets for high-impression, low-CTR queries without misrepresenting the offer.
- Publish one fact-checked guide only when the process, platform settings, and sources are confirmed.
- Earn relevant mentions through genuine partnerships and useful resources; do not buy links or automate outreach.

## Measurement

- **Search visibility:** impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, query/page pairs, indexed canonical pages.
- **Lead quality:** completed enquiries, qualified adult eligibility, response rate, and privacy complaints.
- **Experience:** mobile usability and field data for Core Web Vitals.
- Do not use page views alone as the success measure.

## AI/GEO/AEO

Google states AI search uses standard search fundamentals and has no separate guaranteed AI optimization. Maintain accessible HTML, clear identity, evidence-backed answers, technical crawlability, and accurate structured data. `llms.txt` is an optional navigation convention only.

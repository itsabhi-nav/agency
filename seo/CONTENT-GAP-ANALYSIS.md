# Content gap analysis

## Existing coverage (verified in source)

- `/`: service overview, requirements, how to start, privacy, safety, earnings estimates, videos, FAQs, and calls to action.
- `/apply`: application flow, eligibility and next steps.
- `/help`: contact options, privacy and safety, agency switching, and FAQs.
- `/llms.txt`: concise, domain-independent site guide.

## Useful additions, conditional on evidence

| Opportunity | Intent | Priority | Preconditions |
|---|---|---:|---|
| “How creator onboarding works” explainer | Informational / commercial | High | Verify each step against current official platform rules and the agency's actual process. Clearly disclose agency vs platform roles. |
| Privacy settings guide | Informational | High | Verify setting names, behavior, availability, and limitations in current product UI. Do not promise anonymity or complete blocking. |
| Payment process and records explainer | Commercial / trust | High | Obtain written confirmation of payout schedule, method, eligibility, fees, documentation, and any agency/platform split. |
| Agency-switch checklist | Informational / commercial | Medium | Confirm current platform terms, account ownership, lock-in restrictions, and lawful process. |
| Creator safety / scam reporting guide | Informational | Medium | Use current official platform, government, and cybercrime sources; review with a qualified local legal/compliance adviser. |

Do not publish a topic until the agency can support its claims. Put the reader's question first, give a short direct answer, then explain steps and limitations with sources.

## Not recommended

- City/state landing-page clones with only a location swapped.
- Generic “jobs for women” pages that conceal the adult-creator nature of the work.
- Guaranteed earnings, employment, anonymity, safety, or legal-status statements.
- Fake case studies, reviews, credentials, official status, or legal advice.
- FAQ markup solely to seek rich-result eligibility.
- Article volume targets or thin AI-generated pages.

## Existing content trust review required

The website includes “verified/official” positioning, privacy absolutes, earnings ranges, tax/payment claims, and account-safety assertions. The agency owner should verify documentation and qualify/remove any claim that cannot be substantiated. This report does not determine legal compliance.
